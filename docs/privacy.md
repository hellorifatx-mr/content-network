# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

This application ("the App") is a private, internal publishing tool operated by a
single individual to publish that individual's own photos and videos to social
media accounts that the individual owns and controls.

## What the App does
The App reads media files from the operator's own Google Drive and publishes them
to the operator's own Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, Pinterest boards, TikTok
account and YouTube channels using each platform's official API.

## Data collected
The App has no users other than the operator. It collects no data from any third
party. It stores only: the operator's own media metadata (file names, captions),
the identifiers of the operator's own accounts, and the identifiers of posts the
App itself created.

## Access tokens
Access tokens are stored encrypted in GitHub Actions Secrets and are used solely
to publish the operator's own content. They are never shared or sold.

## Data retention and deletion
Publishing records are retained for operational history. To request deletion of
all data, or to revoke the App's access, contact the address below; access can
also be revoked at any time from each platform's app settings page.
